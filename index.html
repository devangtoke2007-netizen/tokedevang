ASL Sign Guide

I Love You

Gesture

Thumb, index, and pinky fingers extended up; middle and ring fingers curled down.

Hello

Gesture

Open flat hand near forehead / side of head as a salute gesture.

Peace

Gesture

Index and middle fingers extended in a V shape; others curled.

Name

Gesture

Index and middle fingers extended straight on both hands, tapping together horizontally.

Thank You

Gesture

Flat hand touches chin and moves forward outward.

Sorry

Gesture

Closed fist making a circular motion against the center of the chest.

Please

Gesture

Flat open palm rubbing in a circular motion on the chest.

Love

Gesture

Both hands crossed over the chest in a fist.

Bye

Gesture

Open hand raised up near shoulder, fingers waving forward and backward.
